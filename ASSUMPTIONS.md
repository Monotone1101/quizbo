## Dependencies beyond `stack.md`

- **AI provider:** `stack.md` specifies the Anthropic Claude API; at the client's request every AI touchpoint uses **Google Gemini** instead, through `@google/genai`, defaulting to `gemini-3.8-flash` (the newest model with a free tier). Free-tier prompts may be used by Google to improve its products — use a paid key for real students.
- **Tooling:** `turbo` (monorepo tasks), `tsx` (runs the TypeScript battle service and worker without a bundler), `vitest`.
- **Prisma 7 requirements:** `@prisma/adapter-pg` + `pg` (driver adapters are mandatory in v7), `dotenv` for `prisma.config.ts`.
- **Web UI:** `radix-ui` (the primitives shadcn/ui wraps), `class-variance-authority`, `clsx`, `tailwind-merge`, `sonner` (toasts), `lucide-react` (icons, as the design specifies), `server-only`.
- **Validation/auth:** `zod` (form + LLM output schemas), `jose` (battle tokens shared by web and battle service), `@auth/prisma-adapter`.
- shadcn/ui components are written in the shadcn pattern and restyled to Industry rather than generated by the CLI.

## Product and rules decisions

**Battle**
- Damage: correct = 10, +5 under 4 s (speed bonus), +3 from the third consecutive correct answer; wrong = 8 to yourself; no answer = 5 to yourself. HP 100. The streak bonus is an addition to the spec's scoring.
- Rounds are synchronised in time but each player sees a different question: player B's order is player A's order composed with a random derangement, so the same question never appears in the same round for both (features.md §1.2 acceptance). Option order is shuffled per player too.
- Answer time is measured by the server; any client `timeTakenMs` is ignored. 400 ms of latency allowance after the visible deadline.
- After max questions: higher HP wins, then more correct answers, then less total answer time; otherwise a **draw** (ELO actual score 0.5).
- Pause: the round restarts with a fresh full timer for both players. A player who had already answered keeps that answer.
- If both players drop and neither returns within the grace period, the room is abandoned with no result and no rating change.
- Leaving mid-battle (the LEAVE button) is an immediate forfeit — no confirmation dialog, per "no friction in the battle loop".
- A battle needs at least 5 validated questions in scope; it runs min(15, pool) rounds.

**ELO and matchmaking**
- The 0.5 forfeit discount applies to the **winner's gain only**. The player who dropped takes the full loss, so rage-quitting a losing match is never cheaper than finishing it.
- Placement bands from onboarding confidence 1–5: 1050 / 1125 / 1200 / 1275 / 1350.
- Two queued players pair when their gap fits the *wider* of their two bands; the longest-waiting player picks first. After 60 s the closest available player is taken regardless of band. Queues are per subject and per topic (topic rematches only meet other topic rematches).

**Mastery and breakdown**
- Mastery update: exponential moving average toward the battle's accuracy on that topic, learning rate min(0.6, 0.15 × answers). Starting prior from onboarding confidence (0.3–0.7).
- Verdicts: STRONG ≥ 75 %, FAIR ≥ 50 %, WEAK below. Weak-spot ties go to the sub-topic with more answers. Review estimate: 6 minutes per miss.
- The AI weak-spot paragraph is cached once generated; failures fall back to a deterministic paragraph and are retried on a later visit.

**Planner**
- Relative dates are always `inferred` (including "the 18th", which needs a month) so the student confirms them. A draft without a date can't be confirmed until one is added.
- Priority "not stated" is saved as MEDIUM, and the card says so.
- Priority multipliers: HIGH 1.5, MEDIUM 1.0, LOW 0.6. Sessions are 10–45 minutes (the last block of a day may stretch to 50), filled day by day by giving each slot to the topic furthest behind its allocation. A topic below 0.5 mastery ends with a 1v1 battle check-in session.
- Several concurrent exams share each day's budget, weighted by priority × average topic need × urgency (1 + 3 / days left). Minutes already done or skipped today are subtracted when re-planning mid-day.
- Re-plans happen when an exam is confirmed or removed, when the daily budget changes, on the **RE-PLAN** button (offered when mastery is newer than the plan), and nightly in the worker. Pending sessions from today on are replaced; done and skipped ones are kept.
- Topic matching: exact → chapter (`unit`) → all words contained → fuzzy (≥ 0.82) → new topic, plus a small alias table ("light" → optics). The card shows each match before anything is written. An unknown subject is created on confirmation for planning only (it has no battles).
- Skipping with no later session for that topic reassigns nothing; the toast says how many minutes were left unplaced.

**Coach and notes**
- Uploads: PDF or plain text up to 4 MB, sent to Gemini as inline file data and not stored. Cards come back as drafts; only **SAVE TO QUICK NOTES** writes a note.
- The coach drawer overlays whichever app page is open (the prototype jumped back to the dashboard).

**Design deviations**
- Start-match meta shows the live queue size ("3 in queue now") instead of "avg wait 14s", which isn't measured.
- The sidebar resources block is now **Loot drops**: the study links you opened most recently (remembered in this browser), falling back to picks for your weakest topics. The sidebar toggle is a muted ghost icon.
- Added to the start panel: **Invite a friend** and **Join with code** (invite rooms were referenced but not designed). Onboarding, sign-in, settings and the review page are new screens in the Industry style.
- The breakdown's footnote says trends "belong on a progress tab" (that tab isn't built).
- Layouts are fluid and stack below ~1280 px; the 1440 px frame is matched at desktop width. No dedicated mobile design yet.

**Palette and motion**
- The Industry steel-blue palette is replaced by the brand palette: Imperial Blue #0A2463, Blue Bell #3E92CC, Ghost White #FFFAFF, Magenta Bloom #D8315B and Carbon Black #1E1B18.
  - Day mode: Ghost White background, Imperial Blue actions.
  - Night mode: Carbon Black background, Blue Bell actions.
  - Magenta marks heat: damage, weak spots and streaks.
- Buttons and search fields have a glowing spectrum along their bottom edge.
- `GridPulse` (`components/ui/grid-pulse.tsx`) lights up behind the sign-in hero, the Resources header and the battle arena.
- All motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

**Boosts** (`packages/core/src/boosts.ts`, applied by the battle service)

| Boost | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Bubble Shield | Blocks the next damage you would take. |
| Double Tap | Your next correct answer does double damage. |
| Fifty-Fifty | Removes two wrong options from this question. |
| Time Warp | Adds 5 s to your clock on this question. |
| Med Kit | Restores 15 HP. |
| Mirror Coat | Half the next hit you take bounces back. |
| Streak Anchor | Your next miss doesn't break your streak. |
| Vampire Bite | Heals you for half the damage your next correct answer deals. |
| Ink Splat | Covers the rival's question with ink for 3 s. |
| Overclock | The speed bonus stays on for this whole question. |

- **Deal:** each player gets 3 distinct boosts per battle.
- **Timing:** one boost per question, fired before answering.
- **Visibility:** armed boosts are visible to both players.
- **Order:** Bubble Shield is checked before Mirror Coat, and a reflected hit can knock out the attacker.
- **Ink Splat is client-side only:** a modified client could ignore it. Every other effect is applied on the server.

**JEE study library** (`/resources`, `packages/db/src/content/jee.ts`)
- Topics follow the JEE Main syllabus, with the existing question-bank topics slotted in. Subject level changed to **Class 11–12**, and a **Chemistry** subject was added.
- Links are free, openly licensed textbooks (OpenStax, HyperPhysics) and the official exam sites. Every link was fetched and its page checked for an expected phrase (`npm run resources:check -w @quizbo/db`). Khan Academy was left out because its bot wall blocks that check. The "Experimental skills" unit was left out because no page for it could be verified.
- NCERT and JoSAA don't respond reliably to requests from outside India, so the automated check skips them. Their URLs are the official ones.
- New topics have no battle questions yet: Chemistry is absent from the subject switcher, and "Battle this" appears only on topics with at least 5 validated questions. Run the question pipeline to fill them.
- Design deviation: a **Resources** link was added to the top nav, and the sidebar resources header links to the library ("ALL →").

## Known gaps

- Single battle-service instance (in-memory rooms and queue); Redis-backed rooms are the documented follow-up.
- No anti-cheat beyond independent question/option orders and server-side timing (tab-switch detection and single-session enforcement are out of MVP scope). Opening the battle in a second tab moves the session there.
- The seed question bank needs an educator's spot-check before launch.
- No multi-battle progress tab, no mobile-specific layouts, no AI-sourced resources job yet.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` is honoured by the canvases, video and button motion; there's no in-app motion toggle.
